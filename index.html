About Rixle

Structured Solutions for the Circular Industrial Economy

Rixle Private Limited addresses the core feedstock bottleneck facing global processors: unreliable quality and untraceable supply chains.

We combine heavy logistics infrastructure with cutting-edge optical classification algorithms. By treating commercial waste streams as structured resource deposits, we ensure steady, compliant feedstock for manufacturing off-takers while guaranteeing regulatory ease for generators.

EPR Assurance: Every metric ton processed through our facilities generates tamper-proof digital verification for Central and State Pollution Control Board filings.

Proprietary Technology

AIRWSS — AI-Assisted Segregation Pipeline

Developed alongside our tech partner Avency EcoSol, the AIRWSS platform combines high-speed optical vision algorithms with robotic pneumatic actuators.

  • Optical Polymer Identification: Real-time neural network categorization of polymers down to resin type and color grade.
  • Automated Ejection: Multi-channel pneumatic jet arrays operating at millisecond intervals for precision separation.
  • Purity Maximization: Reduces human manual sorting dependency while elevating batch purity scores to over 98%.
