Recovery Flow

From Waste to Recovered Material

A practical recovery workflow from incoming material through sorting, preparation and downstream supply.

1. Collection

Waste received from applicable sources.

2. Receiving

Material inspection and handling.

3. Sorting

Segregation by material category.

4. Preparation

Baling or preparation where applicable.

5. Quality Review

Batch-level material assessment.

6. Storage

Prepared material held for dispatch.

7. Downstream Recycling

Supply into appropriate recycling chains.

8. Circular Supply

Recovered material returns to industry.
