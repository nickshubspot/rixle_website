Pioneering Industrial Sustainability

Rixle Ecosol integrates state-of-the-art computer vision, deep learning, and high-speed robotic actuation to redefine material recovery infrastructure. We process industrial recyclables—including PET bottles, HDPE drums, LDPE films, and PP scrap—into high-purity recycled granules and raw materials.

  • Automated AI Sorting (AIRWSS): High-precision real-time polymer and fraction segregation.

End-to-End Circular Workflow

How material flows from raw industrial waste into high-grade circular commodities

1. Collection

Bulk waste pickup from industrial sources.

2. Transport

GPS-tracked route delivery to MRF.

3. Sorting

Mechanical pre-separation on conveyor.

4. AI Detection

5. Baling

Automated hydraulic compression.

6. Storage

7. Recycler

Washing, flaking, and granulation.
