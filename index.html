Solving the Industrial Feedstock Deficit

The Industry Challenge

Unreliable Feedstock & Regulatory Risk

Industrial manufacturers and plastic recyclers suffer from inconsistent material quality, unorganized collection networks, and incomplete compliance paperwork. Unsegregated waste leads to landfill leakage and severe EPR regulatory audit liabilities.

The Rixle Solution

Structured, Tech-Enabled Recovery

Rixle transforms unorganized waste into standardized industrial feedstock. Through our MRF processing infrastructure and AIRWSS AI-robotic sorting technology, we provide traceable supply chains and audit-ready EPR documentation for corporate clients.

Technology & Innovation

AIRWSS — AI-Robotic Waste Sorting System

Developed through our technology arm, Avency EcoSol, AIRWSS replaces manual sorting limitations with high-speed computer vision and robotic pneumatic separation.

Inquire About AIRWSS Integration

Vision AI Models

Deep-learning neural networks trained to identify plastics by polymer type, color, and contamination level in real time.

Robotic Actuation

Automated pneumatic ejection systems ensuring ultra-fast separation of target recyclables from bulk waste streams.

Purity Optimization

Dramatically increases batch purity rates, enabling higher market value for processed feedstock.

Future Ready

Scalable modular design built for direct deployment in industrial MRFs and municipal sorting lines.
