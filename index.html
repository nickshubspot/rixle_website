R&D Initiative · Under Active Development · Co-Developed with Avency EcoSol

AIRWSS — AI-Assisted Waste Sorting System

Developing next-generation optical machine vision models and pneumatic actuation to upgrade Material Recovery Facilities from manual triage to automated polymer segregation.

AIRWSS Prototype Optical Scanning Module · R&D Laboratory Installation
Industry Challenge

The Feedstock Purity Bottleneck

Industrial plastic recyclers and reprocessors require clean resin feedstock. Commingled municipal and commercial scrap streams contain heavy polymer mixing (PET, HDPE, PP, LDPE) that prevents direct extrusion without high-precision sorting.

Operational Limits

Why Manual Sorting Falls Short

  • Fatigue & Throughput: Manual sorting speed drops significantly over multi-shift operational cycles.
  • Visual Ambiguity: Human operators cannot reliably distinguish transparent PET from transparent PVC or PP films at high belt speeds.
  • Safety Exposure: Direct hand sorting exposes personnel to sharp contaminants, heavy dust, and hazardous materials.
Technical Overview

Automated Optical Identification & Pneumatic Actuation

AIRWSS is designed as a modular conveyor retrofit. Overhead RGB camera arrays capture real-time surface spectral data, passing frames to edge neural networks that trigger targeted high-pressure pneumatic air valves to eject specific resin fractions.

UNDER DEVELOPMENT · CONCEPT SCHEMATIC

Hardware-Software Loop

1. Material enters via conveyor $\rightarrow$ 2. Scanned under calibrated lighting $\rightarrow$ 3. Edge AI predicts resin class $\rightarrow$ 4. Millisecond valve burst ejects target item into Accept chute.

01

Image Capture

High-resolution industrial camera array capturing frames under calibrated 5000K LED illumination.

02

Pre-Processing

Background subtraction, lens distortion correction, and region-of-interest (ROI) segmentation.

03

Inference Engine

Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) evaluating surface geometry, color resin, and transparency.

04

Actuation Delay Calculation

Encoder-linked spatial tracking mapping item velocity to pneumatic jet coordinate trigger time.

Technical Inquiries

AIRWSS R&D Frequently Asked Questions

Is AIRWSS currently available for commercial purchase?

No. AIRWSS is currently an active R&D technology initiative co-developed with Avency EcoSol. It is undergoing prototype testing and is not yet commercially deployed.

Can AIRWSS be retrofitted onto existing MRF conveyor belts?

Yes. The architectural design targets modular mounting over standard industrial sorting conveyors (600mm - 1200mm belt widths) without requiring complete line replacement.

What materials is AIRWSS being trained to recognize?

Initial model training focuses on major rigid and flexible polymer classes including PET (clear/amber), HDPE (blow-molded containers), LDPE film, and Polypropylene (PP) packaging scrap.
