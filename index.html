Industrial Process Architecture

10-Stage Material Recovery Flow

Our multi-stage MRF ecosystem transforms heterogeneous industrial waste into mill-grade secondary raw materials through systematic triage, mechanical separation, and quality control.

Stage Progress: 01 / 10 Use Left/Right Arrow Keys to Navigate Stages
Stage 01

Industrial Waste Collection

Scheduled evacuation of segregated or mixed industrial scrap from manufacturing premises using specialized heavy logistics fleets.

InputUnprocessed Factory Scrap
OutputSecured Ingress Load
Stage 02

Weighbridge & Registration

Gross vehicle weight verification on certified weighbridge platforms, generating digital receipts and shipment tracking ledgers.

InputArriving Vehicle
OutputCertified Tonnage Log
Stage 03

Waste Inspection & Triage

Triage floor screening where quality personnel isolate non-conforming items, hazardous materials, and moisture contamination.

InputBulk Scrap Stream
OutputScreened Triage Feed
Stage 04

Primary Manual Segregation

Incline conveyor feeding to manual presort lines where operators extract large cardboard sheets, bulky plastics, and foreign debris.

InputRaw Conveyor Stream
OutputPresorted Fractions
Stage 05

Mechanical Processing (Trommel Screen)

Rotary trommel drums and air classifiers separate material fractions by dimensional size and weight density.

InputCommingled Presorted Stream
OutputSized Material Fractions
Stage 06 · Under Development

AIRWSS AI Optical Sorting

Proprietary computer-vision platform under R&D designed to classify polymer resins and actuate high-speed pneumatic air jets.

InputMixed Polymer Stream
Output (Target)Resin-Categorized Stock
Stage 07

Baling & Quality Inspection

Sorted material fractions are compacted into high-density industrial bales using hydraulic presses, wire-bound, and inspected.

InputSorted Loose Scrap
OutputHigh-Density Standard Bales
Stage 08

Warehouse Stacking & Indexing

Quality-checked bales are barcoded by resin grade, weight, and origin date, then stored in designated dry warehouse bays.

InputCertified Bales
OutputIndexed Warehouse Inventory
Stage 09

Material Trading & Dispatch

Outbound commercial fulfillment to authorized reprocessors, spinning mills, and manufacturing buyers with manifest documentation.

InputAllocated Stock Bales
OutputOutbound Shipment
Stage 10

Circular Economy Recirculation

Recovered secondary raw materials are reintroduced into manufacturing production cycles, closing the material loop.

InputSecondary Commodities
OutputCirculated Products
