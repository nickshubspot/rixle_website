The Industrial Reality

Manual Waste Processing Is Failing Industrial Supply Chains.

High contamination rates, escalating landfill tipping fees, strict MPCB/CPCB regulatory fines, and volatile recycled material purity continue to cripple commercial waste operations across India.

"Without automated optical sorting at the Material Recovery Facility level, over 65% of recyclable high-value polymers end up incinerated or landfilled."

High Contamination

Manual sorting achieves under 70% purity, leading to rejected material bales and severe pricing discounts.

EPR Non-Compliance

Unorganized waste collectors fail to provide audit-ready EPR certificates or digital chain-of-custody logs.

Operational Bottlenecks

Labor dependency creates throughput limits, restricting facility expansion and bulk off-take capability.

Value Leakage

Unsegregated polymer fractions (PET, HDPE, PP) miss out on premium industrial granulator margins.
