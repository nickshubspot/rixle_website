About Rixle

Building Institutional Waste Recovery Infrastructure

Rixle Pvt Ltd operates at the intersection of waste recovery, secondary material supply, EPR compliance support, and environmental technology R&D.

Founded to structure India's dry waste management ecosystem, Rixle has established an operational sourcing footprint across 9 states. We connect bulk waste generators with Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) and verified downstream recyclers, ensuring material purity, weight verification, and regulatory compliance.

Our business is built on physical operational capability, transparent weighbridge records, and long-term material supply agreements with industrial buyers.
