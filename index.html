Our Circular Recovery Workflow

A systematic, closed-loop approach to waste segregation, material refinement, and distribution.

01

Collection & Intake

Systematic sourcing and transportation of post-consumer and commercial dry waste to MRF centers.

02

Automated Sorting

High-speed classification separating plastic polymers, paper, and metals according to resin codes.

03

Compaction & Baling

Hydraulic pressing of sorted fraction streams into dense bales for efficient storage and transport.

04

Supply & Re-entry

Direct dispatch of purified plastic flakes and bales to industrial manufacturing plants for remanufacturing.
