10-Stage Material Recovery Flow

01

Fleet Ingress

Weighbridge logging & gross mass balance verification.

02

Tipping & Triage

Bulk volume inspection & hazardous fraction isolation.

03

Bag Opening

Enterprise FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Rixle support corporate Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)?

Rixle manages end-to-end collection, segregation, and processing of designated plastic packaging streams. Every ton processed is documented through CPCB portal registrations, generating verifiable EPR credit transfers for Brand Owners and Producers.

What is the AIRWSS sorting platform?

AIRWSS (AI-Robotics Waste Sorting System) is a computer-vision optical sorting platform developed with Avency EcoSol. It retrofits onto MRF conveyor lines to automatically categorize and pneumatically separate polymers by resin type and color grade.

What raw material fractions does Rixle supply to buyers?

We supply baled and shredded secondary raw materials including PET, HDPE, LDPE, PP, OCC paper, industrial textile scrap, and crushed glass cullet, sorted to precise purity specifications.
