About Rixle

A supply chain company for recyclable materials.

Rixle Private Limited was founded to solve a specific problem: industrial and municipal recyclers need consistent feedstock quality and reliable documentation, not just collection volume. We built our operations — collection, sorting, MRF processing and EPR support — around that requirement.

Our network spans multiple states and covers plastics, textile waste, and RDF production, supported by AIRWSS, our AI-assisted sorting layer developed through our technology arm, Avency EcoSol.

"Rixle exists to make recycling operationally reliable — for the businesses generating waste and for the recyclers who need clean, well-documented material to work with."

— Founder's Note
